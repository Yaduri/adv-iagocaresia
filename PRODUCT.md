# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and JavaScript (ES modules), no framework and no build step. Deployed on Cloudflare Pages with automatic deploys from GitHub `Yaduri/adv-iagocaresia` (branch `main`). The Pages build only copies `index.html` and `assets/` into `dist/`.

## Users

Primary: business owners and managers in Tatuí/SP and the Sorocaba region who face tax questions (dívida ativa, execução fiscal, planejamento tributário, Reforma Tributária). They look for a lawyer they can trust, usually on mobile, often coming from the firm's Instagram or a Google search, and want to reach the office quickly by WhatsApp.

Secondary: individuals with consumer/bank contract problems, labor issues (acidente de trabalho), health-plan denials, or income-tax exemption for serious illness (IR por doença grave).

## Product Purpose

One-page presence for Advocacia Caresia (Dr. Iago Caresia Rodrigues, OAB/SP 482.856, Tatuí/SP): present the office, its three official areas (Tributário, Cível, Trabalhista), and convert visitors into WhatsApp conversations or office visits. Success is a qualified WhatsApp message or a scheduled consultation.

## Positioning

The Dr. Iago holds an MBA in Gestão Tributária (USP/ESALQ) and is a specialist in tax law, which a typical small-town general-practice firm cannot truthfully claim. Tax is the flagship area; civil and labor are complementary.

## Product Principles

- Specialist credibility first: tax expertise (USP/ESALQ) leads; civil and labor support it.
- Trust through proof that exists: real photos, real reviews, real address, no invented numbers.
- Compliance by design: informational tone, no outcome promises, visible OAB number.
- One action: every section ends in a WhatsApp path that is short and topic-aware.
- Mobile-first: the audience arrives from Instagram and Google on phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Portuguese (pt-BR). Target WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard-operable tabs, menu and carousel, and `prefers-reduced-motion` respected. No product-specific requirement beyond that was established.
